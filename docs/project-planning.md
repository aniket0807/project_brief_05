Project Title - Inventory Management System

Problem Statement –

What problem exists? 

Many businesses still manage inventory manually through spreadsheets or paper registers, with no real-time sync between stock, sales, and procurement data. This causes stockouts, overstocking, lost items, and inaccurate records.

Who experiences the problem?

Small and medium-sized businesses, warehouse staff, procurement teams, and business owners who lack access to affordable, reliable inventory software and must rely on error-prone manual tracking.

Why does it need to be solved?

Inaccurate inventory directly hurts profitability — lost sales from stockouts, tied-up capital from overstocking, and poor decisions from unreliable data. As operations scale, manual tracking becomes unsustainable, making an automated, real-time IMS essential for efficiency and growth.

Objective –

To design and develop a centralized Inventory Management System that provides real-time tracking of stock levels across purchases, sales, and storage.

To reduce manual errors and operational overhead by automating stock updates, low-stock alerts, and purchase/sales record-keeping.

To enable data-driven decision-making by generating reports and analytics on stock movement, supplier performance, and inventory turnover.

Functional Requirements –

Secure login and logout for authorized users. 

Product Management 

Add, update, delete, and view product details.

Organize products into different categories. 

Inventory Management 

Track and update stock levels in real time. 

Supplier Management

Non-Functional Requirements –

  Performance 

The system should process user requests and update inventory records quickly with minimal response time. 

  Security 

Only authorized users should be able to access the system through secure login and role-based access control. 

  Reliability 

The system should provide accurate inventory data and operate consistently without frequent failures. 

  Usability 

The application should have a simple, user-friendly interface that is easy to learn and use. 

  Scalability 

The system should be capable of handling an increasing number of products, users, and transactions as the business grows. 

  Availability 

The system should be accessible whenever required, with minimal downtime. 

  Maintainability 

The system should be easy to maintain, update, and enhance with new features in the future. 

  Data Integrity 

The system should ensure that inventory records remain accurate, consistent, and free from duplication. 

  Backup and Recovery 

The system should support regular data backups and allow recovery of data in case of system failure. 

  Compatibility 

The application should run smoothly on common operating systems and modern web browsers.

Core Modules

Authentication & User Management Module – Handles login, role assignment, and access control for Admin, Inventory Manager, Staff, and Procurement Officer. 

Product/Inventory Management Module – Manages product creation, categorization, and real-time stock level tracking. 

Purchase Management Module – Handles purchase order creation, supplier linkage, and incoming stock verification. 

Sales/Order Management Module – Records sales transactions and automatically updates stock levels. 

Supplier Management Module – Maintains supplier records, contact details, and order history. 

Stock Alerts & Notifications Module – Monitors stock levels and triggers low-stock/reorder alerts. 

Reporting & Analytics Module – Generates dashboards and reports on stock valuation, turnover, and sales trends. 

Search & Filter Module – Enables quick lookup of products, orders, and transaction history across the system. 

Data Export Module – Allows reports and records to be exported in CSV/Excel format.

Project Scope –

The Inventory Management System is designed to automate and streamline inventory operations by managing products, stock levels, suppliers, purchases, and sales. It provides secure user authentication, real-time inventory tracking, automatic stock updates, low-stock alerts, and report generation to improve inventory accuracy and business decision-making. The system is intended for small and medium-sized businesses and focuses on efficient inventory control. Advanced features such as online payments, e-commerce integration, barcode/RFID support, multi-warehouse management, and AI-based forecasting are beyond the scope of this project and can be implemented in future enhancements.

Expected Outcome –

The Inventory Management System is expected to provide businesses with a centralized, automated platform for tracking stock, purchases, and sales in real time. Upon completion, the system should reduce manual errors in inventory record-keeping, minimize stockouts and overstocking through timely low-stock alerts, and streamline purchase and sales workflows through role-based access. It should also provide actionable insights via reports and analytics, enabling better decision-making around procurement and inventory planning. Overall, the system aims to improve operational efficiency, save time, and reduce the costs associated with manual inventory management.
